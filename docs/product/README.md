# Product Vision & Statutory Framework

**Application:** Digital Impersonation Response Desk  
**Target Domain:** Enterprise Cybersecurity, Synthetic Media (Deepfake) Incident Response, and Legal Brand Protection  
**Target Market:** India B2B Enterprises, Healthcare Networks, Financial Institutions, and High-Profile Executives  

---

## Overview

The Digital Impersonation Response Desk addresses the rapid weaponization of generative AI: voice clones, video deepfakes, spoofed executive social accounts, and fraudulent customer support handles used for financial fraud and corporate extortion.
