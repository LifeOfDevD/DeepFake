## 1. The Real-World Problem

With the proliferation of multimodal generative AI, corporate impersonation has evolved from simple spoofed email accounts to **sophisticated synthetic media campaigns**:
* Voice-cloned CFOs directing emergency wire transfers via messaging apps.
* Deepfake video endorsements of fraudulent pharmaceuticals or fake crypto exchanges using recognizable doctors and founders.
* Coordinated fake customer support handles harvesting credentials from distressed consumers on Telegram, WhatsApp, and X.

Traditional intellectual property tools are too slow, often taking weeks to formulate notices while scammers inflict financial and reputational damage within hours.

---

## 2. The Solution: Digital Impersonation Response Desk

The Response Desk bridges cybersecurity response, digital evidence forensics, and Indian statutory grievance mechanisms:
1. **Accelerated Intake & Normalization:** Ingests signals across social platforms, normalizes URLs, and strips tracking tokens.
2. **72-Hour Statutory Takedown Clocks:** Enforces Indian statutory timelines under Rule 3(2) of the IT Rules 2021 (mandatory 24-hour acknowledgement and 72-hour resolution for impersonation grievances).
3. **Forensic Evidence Locker:** Streaming SHA-256 custody, tamper-evident metadata, and 180-day WORM preservation under Section 65B Indian Evidence Act / Section 63 BSA standards.
4. **Structured Platform Playbooks:** Out-of-the-box legal notice formulations for 9 global platforms (Meta, Instagram, YouTube, X, Telegram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Google, GitHub).
5. **Human Review Governance:** Enforces mandatory multi-role sign-offs before any complaint packet is formatted for delivery.
